Refund Policy

Last Updated: December 30, 2025

1. Subscription Cancellations

You may cancel your SignalCore Data subscription at any time. Cancellations will take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. To cancel, please log in to your account and navigate to the "Billing" section or email us at leads@signalcoredata.com.

2. Refund Eligibility

Due to the proprietary nature of our predictive intelligence, all subscription fees are generally non-refundable. Once data has been delivered or accessed via our platform, the value of the service is considered fulfilled.

3. Exceptional Circumstances

We evaluate refund requests on a case-by-case basis under the following circumstances:

  • Technical failure preventing access to the Service for more than 48 consecutive hours;
  • Billing errors or duplicate charges caused by our platform.

4. Success Fees

Success fees are billed upon the closing of a deal identified via the SignalCore pipeline. Because success fees are contingent on your success, they are strictly non-refundable once paid.

5. Processing Refunds

If a refund is approved, it will be processed to the original method of payment within 5-10 business days. Please note that your financial institution may take additional time to reflect the credit.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about our Refund Policy or wish to discuss an exceptional circumstance, please contact us at leads@signalcoredata.com.
